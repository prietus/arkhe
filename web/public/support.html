Help for Arkhe — a native IRCv3 client for iPhone & iPad

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later.
  • Access to an IRC server or an IRC bouncer.

Getting started

1. Add a server

Tap + in the sidebar and enter your server’s host and port. Enable TLS (recommended). If you use a bouncer, point Arkhe at the bouncer — it will discover the networks available on it.

2. Sign in with SASL

Choose SASL PLAIN and enter your username and password, or use SASL EXTERNAL for certificate‑based login (CertFP).

3. Set up CertFP (optional)

Import a client certificate (.p12), choose SASL EXTERNAL, then copy the SHA‑256 fingerprint Arkhe shows and register it with your network (for example /msg NickServ CERT ADD).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a bouncer?

No. You can connect directly to any IRC server. A bouncer stays connected on your behalf and syncs your history, so you never miss messages and can pick up where you left off across devices — Arkhe supports this via IRCv3 chat history.

Where are my passwords stored?

In the iOS Keychain, encrypted by the system and never in plain text. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I attach an image or file?

Configure a custom uploader in Settings → Upload, then use the attach button in the message bar. Files are sent directly to the endpoint you configure.

How do I close a channel?

Swipe left on the channel in the sidebar and tap Close.
