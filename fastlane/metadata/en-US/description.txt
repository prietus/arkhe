Arkhe is a modern, native IRC client for iPhone and iPad, built from the ground up for IRCv3 and designed to feel right at home with modern IRC bouncers.

Whether you run your own bouncer or connect directly to your favorite IRC networks, Arkhe keeps your conversations in sync and your credentials secure.

WHY ARKHE
• Native SwiftUI app — fast, lightweight and made for iOS.
• Built for IRCv3, not retrofitted onto an old protocol.
• Secure by default: passwords and certificates live in the iOS Keychain, never stored in plain text.

SECURE AUTHENTICATION
• SASL PLAIN for username and password login.
• SASL EXTERNAL with CertFP — sign in with a client TLS certificate, no password required.
• TLS connections with modern cipher support.

BUILT FOR BOUNCERS
• Full IRC bouncer support: automatically discover and browse every network on your bouncer.
• A seamless multi-network experience from a single connection.
• Automatic scrollback with chathistory — pick up right where you left off when you join a channel.

IRCv3 DONE RIGHT
• server-time for accurate timestamps on history.
• message-tags, batch, echo-message, cap-notify.
• account-notify, away-notify, chghost, extended-join, multi-prefix, userhost-in-names, setname and invite-notify.

MADE FOR iPHONE AND iPAD
• Adaptive layout with a three-column view on iPad.
• A clean, focused reading experience.
• Inline media and file uploads through your own uploader.

Arkhe is open source. If you'd rather build it yourself, the full source code is available — see the developer's website.

Note: Arkhe is a chat client. You'll need access to an IRC server or an IRC bouncer to use it.
