• Ignore people: long-press a message or a member, or type /ignore nick. Their messages are hidden and never notify you; /unignore or Edit server → Ignored users brings them back. Ignore by nick, hostmask (with * and ? wildcards) or account.
• On a soju bouncer, ignores, muted and pinned conversations sync with your other clients, and soju stops push notifications from people you ignore.
• Pin conversations to the top of the list, and mute them per server.
• See whether the people you chat with privately are online, away or offline.
• Channel invitations now show up, with a notification, and /invite lets you send them.
• Push notifications on Ergo servers that support them.
• Avatars, display names and pronouns in the member list on servers that share them.
• When you connect straight to a network (no bouncer), the channels you have open are joined again automatically.
